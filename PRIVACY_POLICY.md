# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the "Mercante in Fiera" mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
*   **Player Name:** When you use the App, you may choose a player name. This name is used to identify you in both single-player and multiplayer game sessions.
*   **Settings Preferences:** We store your preferences such as language, theme, and sound settings locally on your device.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
To provide multiplayer functionality and ensure security, we use Google Firebase services, which collect certain information automatically:
*   **Identifiers:** A unique User ID (UID) is generated by Firebase Authentication when you first use the App.
*   **Device Information:** The App may collect information about your device, such as model, operating system version, and IP address.
*   **Log Data:** Basic technical logs related to your connection to our multiplayer servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
*   **To Provide Multiplayer Gameplay:** Your player name and UID are used to synchronize game states and allow you to interact with other players in a "Room."
*   **To Maintain App Preferences:** Your settings (language, sounds, etc.) are stored to provide a consistent user experience.
*   **Security and Fraud Prevention:** Automatically collected device info and identifiers help us prevent abuse and ensure the stability of the multiplayer servers.

## 3. How Your Information is Shared

*   **With Other Players:** Your player name is visible to other players who join the same game room as you.
*   **Service Providers:** We use **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Realtime Database) to host the multiplayer infrastructure. Your data is processed by Google on our behalf according to their privacy terms. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

*   **Multiplayer Data:** Information related to a game room (including player names and status) is transient and is typically removed from the database when the session ends or all players leave.
*   **Account Deletion:** Although the App uses anonymous authentication, you can request the deletion of your data or reset your profile at any time by contacting us at dev@gtpware.eu or by clearing the App's storage/cache in your device settings.

## 5. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All communications between the App and Firebase servers are encrypted using industry-standard TLS (Transport Layer Security).

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our official website.

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at:
dev@gtpware.eu.
